# Personnel Provenance

Documents the source of musician data for each album. "Direct" means the
personnel list was taken from a source specific to that recording. "Inferred"
means the lineup was derived from a related source and may not exactly reflect
who performed on that particular release.

## Inferred

Personnel for these albums was not available on beefheart.com or as a
Wikipedia article for that specific release. The lineup was derived from the
studio album recorded by the same band in the same year.

| Album | Inferred from | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Live London '74 | Unconditionally Guaranteed (1974) | Same touring band, same year |
| I'm Going To Do What I Wanna Do: Live At My Father's Place 1978 | Shiny Beast (Bat Chain Puller) (1978) | Same touring band, same year |
| Amsterdam '80 | Doc At the Radar Station (1980) | Same touring band, same year |

**Caveat:** Touring lineups sometimes differed from studio lineups even within
the same year. These credits should be treated as probable rather than confirmed.

## Inherited via shared songs (no musicians explicitly assigned)

These are multi-era live compilations. No musicians were directly assigned to
them. The personnel that appears against their songs in the database comes
entirely from the song deduplication system — each track on these compilations
matched an existing song record that already carried studio-album personnel.

| Album | Years covered | Personnel comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Railroadism – Live in the US 72-81 | 1972–1981 | Studio album records for matching tracks |
| Magnetic Hands – Live in the UK 72-80 | 1972–1980 | Studio album records for matching tracks |

**Caveat:** Personnel shown for individual tracks on these compilations reflects
the studio lineup for that song, not necessarily who performed the live version
included on the compilation.
